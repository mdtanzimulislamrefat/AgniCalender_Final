Record a screen recording of `web/index.html` with this voiceover. English only.
Speak at a normal pace — about 75 words in 30 seconds. Keep the video to 30 seconds
or less; a 31-second video can be ruled ineligible. Upload to YouTube as Public
(or Unlisted with a public link — the judges' link must not need a login) and paste
that link as the Project Demo link. No NASA logo in the video.
